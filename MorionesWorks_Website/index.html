A LIVING TRADITION — MARINDUQUE

ROOTED IN CULTURE

More than a product.
A piece of our story.

The Moriones Festival is one of Marinduque's most recognized cultural traditions. Its iconic Roman-soldier masks inspire local creativity, craftsmanship, and products treasured by residents, visitors, and collectors.

MorionesWorks brings these creations into one online space while helping organize the work behind every order.
